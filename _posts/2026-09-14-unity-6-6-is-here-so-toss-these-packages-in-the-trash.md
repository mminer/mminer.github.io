---
title: "Unity 6.6 Is Here So Toss These Packages in the Trash"
---

Unity 6.6 is out, and with it a raft of improvements that make two editor utilities I built obsolete. You can now navigate through your editor [selection history](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.6/Documentation/Manual/selection-history.html) --- goodbye [Selection History Navigator](/2018/04/22/unity-selection-history-navigator) --- and the updated hierarchy window now shows [component icons](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.6/Documentation/Manual/hierarchy-reference.html#icons-in-the-hierarchy-window) like my [Hierarchy Icons](/2018/12/31/component-icons-in-unity-hierarchy) package once did.

![Unity hierarchy icons](/images/unity-new-hierarchy-icons.png)

This is good stuff, but you know what I'm really excited for? [Serializable dictionaries](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.6/Documentation/Manual/script-serialization-dictionaries.html). I never thought I'd see the day.
